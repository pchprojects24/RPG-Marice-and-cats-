# API Category Guide

This document provides a brief overview of the categories used to organize the APIs in the `apis.json` dataset. These categories help in filtering and discovering APIs based on their primary function.

---

### Weather & Environment

APIs in this category provide data related to weather forecasts, current conditions, climate information, and environmental data. This includes temperature, precipitation, wind, air quality, and more.

*Examples: Open-Meteo, OpenWeatherMap, Environment Canada*

---

### Geography & Location

This category includes APIs for geocoding (converting addresses to coordinates), reverse geocoding, IP-based geolocation, and retrieving detailed information about countries, cities, and places.

*Examples: REST Countries, Nominatim, IP-API*

---

### Finance & Currency

These APIs offer financial data, including real-time and historical currency exchange rates, stock market data, and cryptocurrency prices.

*Examples: ExchangeRate-API, CoinGecko*

---

### Science & Space

APIs focused on scientific data, including astronomy, space exploration, physics, and earth sciences. This category features data from organizations like NASA, SpaceX, and the USGS.

*Examples: NASA APOD, SpaceX API, Launch Library 2, USGS Earthquakes*

---

### Books & Knowledge

This category is for APIs that provide access to books, articles, research papers, and general knowledge databases. It includes library catalogs, dictionaries, and encyclopedic sources.

*Examples: Open Library, Gutendex, Wikipedia API, PoetryDB*

---

### Media & Images

APIs that serve images, videos, and other media. This includes stock photo providers, placeholder image generators, and museum art collections.

*Examples: Unsplash, Lorem Picsum, Art Institute of Chicago*

---

### Entertainment

This category covers APIs related to movies, TV shows, music, video games, and other forms of entertainment. It also includes APIs for jokes and trivia.

*Examples: The Movie Database (TMDb), TVMaze, Studio Ghibli API, Open Trivia Database*

---

### Government & Public Data

APIs that provide access to open data from government sources at the national, state, and local levels. This includes census data, crime statistics, transit information, and more.

*Examples: Open Government Canada, Statistics Canada, FBI Crime Data API*

---

### Utilities

This category contains APIs that provide useful developer utilities, such as IP address information, chart generation, fake data for testing, and file conversion.

*Examples: IPify, JSONPlaceholder, QuickChart*

---

### Fun / Random APIs

A collection of fun, simple, and often humorous APIs that are great for small projects, demos, or just for fun. This includes random quote generators, joke APIs, and more.

*Examples: icanhazdadjoke, Chuck Norris IO, Kanye.rest, RandomUser*
